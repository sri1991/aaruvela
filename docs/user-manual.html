Section 1

How to Open the Website

You can use the website on any phone, tablet, or computer. You do not need to download any app.

  1. 1
    Open your internet browser

    On a phone this is usually Chrome or Safari. On a computer it could be Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. Tap or click the browser icon to open it.

  2. 2
    Type the website address

    Tap the address bar at the top of the browser (where the website address is shown) and type:

    aaruvela.org  — then press Enter or Go

How to Login to Your Account

To see your member details and ID card you must first login. You will need your mobile number and your PIN (a 4-digit number given to you by the Parishat office).

  1. 1
    Click "Login" in the top menu

    At the top of the page you will see a menu. Look for and click the Login button.

    📷

  2. 2
    Enter your mobile number

    In the box labelled Mobile Number, type your 10-digit mobile number. Do not add +91 or 0 at the beginning — just the 10 digits, for example: 9876543210.

    📷

  3. 3
    Enter your PIN

    In the box labelled PIN, type your 4-digit PIN. The PIN was sent to you when your membership was approved. If you do not know your PIN, please contact the Parishat office.

  4. 4
    Click the "Login" button

    Click the green Login button. If your mobile number and PIN are correct, you will be taken to your Member Dashboard.

Forgot your PIN? Contact the Parishat office. They will reset it for you. Your new PIN will be 0000 — please change it immediately after logging in (see Section 7).
Section 3

Applying for Membership (New Members)

If you are a new member and do not yet have an account, follow these steps to apply.

This section is only for people who do not yet have a member account. If you already have an account, skip to Section 4.
  1. 1
    Go to the Login page and click "Register"

    On the login page, look for a link that says New Member? Apply Here or Register and click it.

    📷
    Screenshot: Login page with Register / Apply link highlighted
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  2. 2
    Choose your membership type

    You will see three options:

    • Permanent – Lifetime membership (one-time fee of ₹5,000)
    • Normal – Regular annual membership (₹100 per year)
    • Associated – For associate/affiliate members

    Click on the type that applies to you.

  3. 3
    Fill in your personal details

    Enter your full name, mobile number, date of birth, address, and other details asked. All fields marked with a * are required.

    📷
    Screenshot: Membership application form
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  4. 4
    Make payment and enter the reference number

    Pay the membership fee by UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) to the Parishat UPI ID shown on screen. After paying, type your UPI Transaction ID in the box provided.

    Important: Save your UPI Transaction ID. You will need it to track your application.
  5. 5
    Submit your application

    Click the Submit Application button. You will see a confirmation screen with your application number. The Parishat office will review your application and activate your account. You will be informed once it is approved.

Section 5

Downloading Your ID Card

You can download your official Parishat ID card as an image and save it on your phone or print it.

  1. 1
    Go to your Dashboard

    Login and go to your Member Dashboard (Section 4).

  2. 2
    Scroll down to your ID card

    Scroll down the page until you can see your ID card displayed on screen.

    📷
    Screenshot: ID card section on the dashboard with the Download button visible below it
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  3. 3
    Click "Download ID Card"

    Below the ID card you will see a green button that says ⬇ Download ID Card. Click or tap it.

  4. 4
    Wait a moment

    A message will appear saying "Generating your official ID card…" — this takes just 2–3 seconds. Please do not tap anything else while it is loading.

  5. 5
    The image will be saved

    A .jpg image file will automatically be downloaded to your phone or computer. On a phone it will appear in your Downloads folder or in your photo gallery depending on your device.

    Tip: You can send this image on WhatsApp or print it at any photo print shop.
Your ID card shows: Your full name · Membership type · Mobile number · Date of birth · Region · Member ID · President's signature
Section 6

Editing Your Profile

You can update your personal information — such as your address, email, or photo — at any time from the dashboard.

  1. 1
    Go to your Dashboard and find Personal Details

    Scroll down past your ID card to the Personal Details section.

  2. 2
    Click "Edit Profile"

    In the top-right corner of the Personal Details section, click the link that says Edit Profile.

    📷
    Screenshot: Personal Details section with "Edit Profile" link highlighted
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  3. 3
    Make your changes

    A form will appear with your current details. Click on any field you want to change and type the new information.

    📷
    Screenshot: Edit Profile form
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  4. 4
    Upload a photo (optional)

    If you want to add or change your profile photo, tap the camera icon or the Upload Photo area. Choose a photo from your phone or computer. A clear front-facing photo works best for your ID card.

  5. 5
    Click "Save Changes"

    When you are done, click the Save Changes button. You will see a green success message when your changes are saved.

Tip: After updating your photo, download a fresh copy of your ID card so it shows your new photo.
Section 7

Changing Your PIN

Your PIN is a 4-digit number used to login. It is important to keep it safe and change it if you think someone else might know it.

  1. 1
    Go to your Dashboard and find Personal Details

    Scroll down past your ID card to the Personal Details section.

  2. 2
    Click "Change PIN"

    Next to "Edit Profile", click the link that says Change PIN.

    📷
    Screenshot: Personal Details section with "Change PIN" link highlighted
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  3. 3
    Enter your new PIN twice

    Type your new 4-digit PIN in the first box. Type the same PIN again in the second box (to confirm). Make sure both boxes show the same number.

    📷
    Screenshot: Change PIN form with two PIN fields
    Replace this box with a screenshot
  4. 4
    Click "Update PIN"

    Click the button to save your new PIN. You will see a confirmation message when it is changed successfully.

Choose a PIN that is:
✓ Easy for you to remember
✓ Not an obvious number like 1234 or 0000
✓ Known only to you — do not share it with others

Section 12

Common Problems & Solutions

Having trouble? Here are answers to the most common issues.

Problem What to do
I cannot login — it says "Invalid credentials" Check that your mobile number has exactly 10 digits and no spaces. Check your PIN carefully. If still not working, contact the Parishat office to reset your PIN.
The page is not loading Check your internet connection. Try closing the browser and opening it again. Make sure you typed the address correctly: aaruvela.org
My photo is not showing on the ID card Go to Edit Profile (Section 6) and upload a photo. After saving, the ID card will update automatically.
The ID card download is not working Make sure you are using Chrome or Safari browser. Allow the browser to download files when it asks. Try again after a few seconds.
I applied for membership but my account is not active Membership applications are reviewed manually by the Parishat office. Please wait 2–3 working days after submitting. Contact the office if it has been longer.
I forgot my PIN Contact the Parishat office. They will reset your PIN to 0000. Login with 0000 and immediately change it (Section 7).
I cannot see the Matrimony / News section These sections require you to be logged in with an active membership. Make sure you are logged in first.
